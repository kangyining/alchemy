# Investment Policy Statement (Extract)
**March 2026**

## 1. Investment Objectives and Function

The Group shall deploy capital in accordance with an investment strategy designed to achieve the key attributes outlined herein, including (1) overall geographic and asset class diversification, sustainable cash-yielding assets, and long-term portfolio appreciation, hereinafter defined as the **Value Preservation Portfolio (VP)**, and (2) preserving flexibility for value creation initiatives and opportunities of UBOs interests, hereinafter defined as the **Value Creation Portfolio (VC)**.

| **Objective** | **Details** |
|---|---|
| **Group Positioning** | Pursue generational wealth preservation and creation through an institutional-grade investment framework, rather than a traditional family office approach. |
| **Asset Allocation** | Diversify portfolio of assets and mitigate risks related to high concentration of capital in real estate and Bahrain. Increase share of non-real estate assets, including direct and indirect financial investments (i.e. public equities, fixed income, alternative investments, etc.). Enhance overall portfolio performance through high-quality direct investments aligned with the Group's core thematics, including lifestyle, sports, media, entertainment, and strategic partnerships. |
| **Sale of Assets** | Monetize and/or release capital from selected real estate assets and redeploy in line with the IPS. |
| **Third Party Capital** | Arrange for third-party capital / co-investors to participate in new large-sized investments and/or dilute Group's position where feasible. |
| **Leverage** | Raise debt to support investment plan and accelerate portfolio diversification. |
| **Distributions** | Target Group portfolio distributions from 2028 onwards with a long-term objective of gross 6% p.a. of VP Portfolio AuM. |

The Group operates through three strategic verticals, structured to reflect the competencies required across asset classes and aligned with the Group's core thematic interests:

| **Vertical** | **Entity** | **Description** |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Real Estate Direct Investments | Maalem | Income-generating and development real estate. |
| 2. Non-Real Estate Direct Investments (Unlisted Equity) | Infinity Ventures | Lifestyle, food and beverage, sports, media, and other investments. |
| 3. Financial Investments | Global Financial Portfolio | Comprising a core and satellite portfolio of public and private financial securities, generally passive in nature. The core portfolio aligns with the Value Preservation strategy (i.e. Infinity Portfolio), while the satellite portfolio accommodates high conviction, higher risk, concentrated, actively managed, and thematic Value Creation oriented investments. |

The Group portfolio is divided into two distinct perimeters to ensure alignment with long-term investment goals and risk management discipline, and is designed around dual primary functions that balance long-term value creation with stable distributions:

- **Value Preservation Portfolio (VP)** — Focused on stable, income-generating, and liquid assets delivering predictable cash flows and capital protection.
- **Value Creation Portfolio (VC)** — Focused on development-phase, growth-oriented, and strategic investments enhancing long-term value and UBO interests.

| **Description** | **Value Preservation (VP)** | **Value Creation (VC)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage / Maturity** | Mature, stabilized, or income-producing assets | Early stage; Growth oriented; Development-phase projects; Strategic investments |
| **New Investment Deployment** | Aligned with top-down Target Asset Allocation | Opportunistic or shareholder-driven; Advance to monetization or transition to VP; not necessarily aligned with Target Asset Allocation |
| **Source of Funding** | Internal Accruals, Exits, and UBO cash contributions from time to time | Based on UBO directives |
| **Operational Involvement** | Mostly no operational role | May require operational management |
| **Gross Annual Cash Return — Expected Gross Distribution, not before 2028 (a)** | 3% | Nil |
| **Gross Annual Capital Growth — Non-Cash Return (b)** | 4–5% | >11% |
| **Gross Annualized Total Return (c) = (a) + (b)** | 7–8% | >11% |
| **Target Gross Distribution (including exit realizations)** | 6% from 2028 onwards | Nil |
| **UBO Liquidity Requirements** | Nil (None outside of Target Distributions outlined above) | Nil |
| **Time Horizon** | Long term (over 10 years) | Long term (over 10 years) |
| **Risk Tolerance** | Low-to-Moderate | Moderate-to-High |

**Additional Notes:**

- *The long-term strategic objective is to increase VP assets and reduce VC exposure to sustain an income-oriented portfolio capable of delivering the Group's 6% annual distribution target on VP Portfolio.*
- *VC assets include exception-based investments of higher risk/return profile and are typically non-yielding.*
- *All opportunities to be screened against VP criteria; non-qualifying yet owner-directed investments (or Group synergy investments i.e. legacy asset swaps) to be identified as VC.*
- *Priority shall be given to VP investments.*
- *Returns are calculated on AuM and are pre-Group Operating Expenses (as defined in Section 11).*

## 2. Long-Term Strategic Diversification Target Asset Class and Geographic Allocation

Infinity Capital shall deploy and/or rebalance the Group's existing portfolio to enhance its diversification and target the asset/geographic allocation below, with ±5–10% deviations being permitted, and noting the concentration parameters and limits outlined herein.

### Asset Class Allocation

| **Asset Class** | **Target %** | **Permitted Range %** |
|---|---|---|
| Financial Investments \| Global Financial Portfolio | 40–45 | ±10 |
| Real Estate (Hospitality, Retail, Residential) \| Maalem | 35–40 | |
| Non-Real Estate Direct Investments (Unlisted Equity) (Lifestyle, F&B, Sports, Media, Entertainment) \| Infinity Ventures | 15–20 | |
| Cash, Cash Equivalents, and Capital Reserve¹ (if any) | 5–7 | ±(5–7) |

*Allocations are estimated based on the annual opening AuM.*

*(1) Capital Reserve refers to ongoing discussions regarding the potential establishment of a dedicated allocation toward gold, silver, and crypto assets.*

### Geographic Allocation

| **Geography** | **Target %** | **Permitted Range %** |
|---|---|---|
| Bahrain | ≤ 50 | ±10 |
| Other GCC (UAE, KSA) | 25–30 | |
| Developed Markets (US, UK, Europe) | 15–20 | ±5 |
| Other Markets | ≤ 5 | |

*Allocations are estimated based on the annual opening AuM.*

Based on the current illustrative business plan, the Group shall aim to achieve the outlined asset allocation diversification and geographic allocation targets within a 5–7-year period. Allocations are to be validated and demonstrated in a business plan that is currently under preparation. The key enablers of the diversification strategy are as follows:

- Sale of assets and active allocation in asset classes other than Bahrain real estate.
- Co-investor syndication and/or dilution of Group exposure.
- Activation and/or monetization of its land bank.
- Utilization of leverage and retention of profits to support the investment and diversification plan.
- Prioritization of VP investments over VC investments.

## 4. Leverage

No leverage shall be permitted without express consent from the Group owners.

Overall, the maximum permissible financing threshold (leverage) for the Group shall be the lower of:

- 30% of Total Assets, or
- 43% of Total Equity.

The threshold outlined herein includes debt at fully owned entities, as well as proportionate debt in companies where the Group holds a significant stake and/or where recourse can be attributed to the Group, while minority and publicly traded holdings are treated as debt-free.

Asset-level leverage contemplated should be assessed and determined for each asset based on its specific cash-flow profile, risk characteristics, and financing conditions.
